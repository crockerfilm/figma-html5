Check a delivery

See every banner in a delivery playing beside its backup, with its weight, length, click-through and package checked. Figma or Google Web Designer banners; nothing leaves your computer.

Still in development. This is an internal tool that's still being tested, and its checks can miss things. A clean result here doesn't mean a delivery is right: check every banner yourself, against the placement's own specs. Provided as is, with no guarantee.
